How detection works here
Rule engine

Honeypot files
Decoy files seeded in every folder. No legitimate process ever touches them — any hit is treated as near-certain, no threshold needed.
Statistical anomaly
A running EWMA baseline + z-score per feature — fully transparent scoring, no black-box model or offline training step.
